01

Camera cleanliness monitoring

2025–present · Computer vision, integration · Professional

A large surveillance deployment had no way to know which of its cameras had degraded — dust, rain, blur, obstruction. Finding out meant someone opening feeds one at a time. I built the system that answers it automatically: frames pulled from the VMS, classified on-server, surfaced as operational state rather than raw model output.

Decision The inference server has no internet access and modest CPU. I trained a lightweight classifier and exported to ONNX rather than shipping a Python runtime — the .NET scanner loads one file and has no dependency chain to vendor.

Validation accuracy landed between 0.76 and 0.88 across runs. That spread is the honest number, and it is why uncertain predictions route to manual review instead of being auto-actioned. Frames were grouped by source video before splitting, because near-duplicate frames across a split will happily invent accuracy that isn't there.

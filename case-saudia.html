Saudia analytics internship

2025 · Power Apps · notification system · NLP prototype · internship

Nine weeks rotating through Saudia's Advanced Data Analytics division — a different department each week, a different project to show for it. Three pieces of that work hold up on their own.

Flight price prediction, shipped inside a Power App

Built individually: a Power App called Flight Booking and Price Tracker, covering flight search, historical price tracking, and prediction. The prediction side trained a Random Forest Regressor on historical fare data, served through a Flask API, and called directly from the app — a full loop from a Kaggle dataset to a number a user could act on inside a real interface, not just a notebook cell.

A price-drop notification concept

A team project with two colleagues: an idea to let customers register a route and get notified when the fare dropped, aimed squarely at revenue and retention. We backed it with a full BRD and demand form, two use-case diagrams, and notification prototypes, then checked the reasoning against real behaviour — a Google Analytics review of the booking funnel showed a large share of users reaching the payment page and leaving without confirming, which is exactly the drop-off a price-drop nudge is meant to recover. The idea went to the Digital Factory's General Manager, who took it further into a discussion about layering a customer-behaviour recommender on top.

A CV-skills classifier prototype

A team project for the HR department, presented as Figma prototype screens rather than a shipped interface. The technical piece behind it was real: an SVM classifier over a TF-IDF vectorisation of extracted CV skills, routing an applicant toward the division their skills best matched.

Recommendation

The internship ended with a letter of recommendation from my supervisor in Advanced Analytics, attesting to Scrum participation, BRD authorship, and the business-analysis work behind the projects above.
